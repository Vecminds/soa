# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Owners and small teams at service businesses (agencies, coaches, consultants, clinics, education and study-abroad consultancies, local services) in Nepal and the USA, plus creators who sell coaching, services or brand deals. Buying questions already reach them in comments and DMs ("price?", "inbox"), spread across Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and others. They visit socioflo.app from a social post or a share, usually on a phone, deciding in under a minute whether to join the waitlist.

## Product Purpose
Socioflo is a pre-product social lead pipeline: it will follow each comment, DM and mention, across platforms, until it becomes a customer, and show which posts brought in the money. The site's job today is to make that idea clear and credible, and convert visitors to the waitlist. Success = waitlist sign-ups from the right businesses, and conversations with early users. Copy says what Socioflo is building, never what it already does.

## Brand Personality
Direct, warm, plain. About people, not metrics. Short sentences. Confident without hype; honest about being in build. The identity's idea is "signals become a person": ring, then half, then the Signal-orange person with a speech tail.

## Anti-references
- Patensify (patensify.com): Socioflo must look clearly distinct from it.
- Instagram-style gradients and platform-brand colours.
- Robot, "24/7 AI agent" and chat-bubble-everywhere imagery.
- Generic SaaS landing templates: gradient text, glow shadows, eyebrow above every section, identical icon-card grids, fake hero metrics.
- The old Socioflo site: green #25A566, Sora, the rocket emoji.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the follow-through.** The page should visibly move from signal to person, the same ring → half → solid progression as the logo.
2. **Honest by design.** Nothing that looks like proof Socioflo doesn't have: sample UI is labelled, no invented numbers, no fake testimonials.
3. **One accent, earned.** Signal orange marks the person / the final stage; everything else is Ink and Paper.
4. **Phone first.** Most visitors arrive from social on a phone; the hero and waitlist form must work there first.
5. **Plain over clever.** Specific nouns and verbs; no buzzwords, no aphorism cadence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI; Signal on Paper only for graphics and 24px+ type. Visible keyboard focus on every interactive element. Every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion alternative; content never depends on a scroll-reveal firing. Platform names in plain text (no third-party logos).
